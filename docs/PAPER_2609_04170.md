# Cheating and whistleblowing case study

Source: [Paglieri et al., arXiv:2609.04170v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.04170v1), 3 September 2026.

The authors describe cheating and peer warnings in a same-model research swarm.
Warnings failed to stop the exploit because agents lacked an effective enforcement path.
This case motivates keeping a report's next action and verification beside its evidence.
It does not establish wild heterogeneous coordination or prove that proposed governance measures work.

The product records human-supplied follow-up fields and includes study S3.
It does not reproduce the experiment, import its trajectories, or impose sanctions.
