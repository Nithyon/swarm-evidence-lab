# Product scope

Swarm Evidence Lab helps a person follow evidence through AI conversations and saved artifacts.
Its graph connects recorded names, source records, and exact references.
A person can inspect a message, move through available date order, and export the visible graph.

The main questions are who responds to whom, whether repeated text represents a new exchange, and what supports a completion claim.
Prepared cases make those distinctions visible without requiring a person to read the full dataset.

The local app reads imported research records. The public app shows invented examples.
Full transcripts, private reviews, and local databases are not included in the public deployment.

The separate experiment screens calculate comparisons from supplied results.
They do not run agents, score transcripts with a model, or claim that collusion detection is solved.
