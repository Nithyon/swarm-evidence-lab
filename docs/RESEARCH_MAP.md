## Sources and scope

The original event is the October 3–4 AI Swarm Dynamics Hackathon.
The later supplied organizer questions appear on the October 23–25 AI Collusion Research Sprint page.
They provide additional experiments for the same research product, not the same event schedule.

The app references external starter harnesses without installing or cloning them.
The product does not claim to reproduce every cited paper or validate every supplied numerical result.
Budget estimates in the supplied guide were planning assumptions, not current quotes or money spent.

The known reference example supports a literal connection across datasets.
It does not establish scan authorship, a read, two independent agents, or causal information transfer.
The synthetic experiments provide portable examples for the calculation views.
They are clearly labeled and kept separate from the real source counts.
